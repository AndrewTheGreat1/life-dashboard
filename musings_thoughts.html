Becoming “Grateful”:

  • If you're ungrateful for what you have, become grateful. Yes, you've heard this before, but you may ask yourself: How does one become “grateful”? Well, try to truly enjoy what you have and remind yourself that things could be worse, but they aren't. It's that simple. Take action now. Yes, you are encountering hardship or unfairness, but that is life sometimes. It's not about fighting through the pain. That will lead to burnout. It's about acknowledging the pain and understanding that it won't last forever. It really won't. It can take years to understand this. Some people are horrible, I know. I want you to understand this now. There is light at the end of the tunnel. As long as you have life in you, there is space for things to improve. Hardship is a part of life (unfortunately, but that is where the knowledge and wisdom are). There is always a bigger picture - something bigger than you, with more detail, colour and background. Always look for the bigger picture. i>

Reinvention is a process. It's a journey, with lessons along the way. It's a series of small steps that lead to big changes. It's about creating new habits and breaking old ones. It's about creating a new identity for yourself. It's about becoming the person you want to be. It's about living the life you want to live. It's about being the best version of yourself. Let's go.
